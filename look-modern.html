Rooms

What kind of rooms does Foley Towers offer?

Every traveller is different, so Foley Towers offers both private and shared rooms. Whether you want a room of your own or a bed alongside fellow travellers, you can find budget accommodation in Christchurch that suits the way you travel.

Private rooms with own facilities

Prefer your own space? Ask about a private room with its own facilities. It gives you a little more privacy and a door to close at the end of the day, while you still stay at a budget backpacker hostel.

Rooms to share with other travellers

Travelling on a budget or keen to meet people? Sharing a room with other travellers is the classic backpacker way to stay. You can swap stories and travel tips with people who are also exploring New Zealand.

Not sure which room to choose?

Tell us your dates and how many people are travelling. Phone or email Foley Towers and we'll let you know which rooms are free.

About

Why stay at a BBH backpacker hostel?

Foley Towers is part of Budget Backpacker Hostels (BBH) New Zealand, the leading New Zealand budget accommodation, hostels and backpackers network. BBH is New Zealand owned and operated, and its aim is simple: to show you the real NZ way.

The BBH guide is the most useful and comprehensive guide dedicated to NZ's network of hostels. BBH also runs online services to help you make the most of your New Zealand visit, including:

  • Online bookings
  • Hostel reviews
  • Work opportunities
  • Vehicle buy/sell notices

Questions travellers ask

Straight answers to help you plan a stay at Foley Towers in Christchurch.

Is Foley Towers budget accommodation?

Yes. Foley Towers is a backpacker hostel in Christchurch and a member of Budget Backpacker Hostels (BBH) New Zealand, the leading budget accommodation network in the country.

Can I get a private room?

Yes. As well as shared rooms, Foley Towers has private rooms with their own facilities. Phone or email to check which rooms are free on your dates.

Can I share a room to keep costs down?

Yes. There are rooms to share with other travellers, the classic backpacker way to stay. It's an easy way to meet people on your New Zealand trip.

What are the reception and check-in times?

Reception and check-in times aren't published here. Please call 03 366 9720 or email foley.towers@backpack.co.nz before you arrive and we'll confirm the details.
